1. Introduction / Background 
Over the past several years, social media has become a central component of how businesses 
communicate with their audiences. For Small and Medium -Sized Enterprises (SMEs), 
maintaining visibility now requires frequent posting, consistent branding, and adher ence to 
platform-specific guidelines. Recent industry findings suggest that AI -based tools are 
increasingly being adopted to assist with repetitive marketing tasks and improve productivity 
in content development [13]. 
Even with these advancements, smaller businesses often struggle to benefit from such 
technologies. Many established platforms offering automation and analytics features operate 
on subscription models that are difficult for SMEs to sustain. Consequently, a large portion of 
this segment continues to depend on manual processes when preparing and reviewing content. 
This reliance on manual handling creates inefficiencies. Tasks such as checking visual 
consistency, selecting effective hashtags, and ensuring compliance with platform policies are 
typically performed independently, often using different tools. This disconnected approach can 
lead to inconsistent outputs, delays in publishing, and increased exposure to policy violations. 
While research and industry reports highlight the growing role of AI in marketing operations 
[14], there remains a lack of solutions that b ring these capabilities together in a cost -effective 
and unified manner. 
 
2. Problem Statement 
Despite progress in AI-driven marketing tools, there is still a noticeable absence of integrated 
systems tailored to the needs and constraints of SMEs. In particular, no widely available 
solution combines automated visual analysis, context -aware hashtag ge neration, and pre -
publication compliance checks within a single, accessible platform. 
Key issues contributing to this gap include: 
• Evaluating visual consistency manually can lead to subjective decisions and 
inconsistent branding, especially when multiple posts are handled over time [15].  
• Many hashtag recommendation systems rely primarily on textual input, overlooking the 
contextual information present in images. Studies indicate that excluding visual 
features can negatively affect the relevance of suggested tags [1].  
• Existing moderation technologies are generally designed for platform enforcement 
rather than for assisting users in reviewing their own content prior to posting [5]. This 
limits the ability of SMEs to proactively avoid violations.

3. Project Contribution 
The proposed system introduces a set of features aimed at addressing the limitations identified 
in both research and industry practices: 
• End-to-End Workflow Integration: 
The platform consolidates several key processes —image analysis, text processing, 
compliance validation, and engagement estimation —into a single environment. This 
reduces the need for switching between multiple tools and simplifies content 
management.  
• Combined Image–Text Understanding: 
Hashtag generation is performed using both visual and textual inputs, enabling outputs 
that better reflect the overall context of the content. This extends beyond approaches 
that depend solely on captions and aligns with recent developments in multimodal 
learning [1][3].  
• Continuous Brand Monitoring: 
Instead of relying on manual inspection, the system evaluates visual elements such as 
color distributions over time to identify inconsistencies. This enables a more systematic 
approach to maintaining brand identity [4].  
• Accessibility-Focused Design: 
The platform is structured to remain affordable and practical for SMEs, particularly in 
regions where access to enterprise-level tools is limited. The goal is to lower the barrier 
to adopting AI-driven marketing support.  
Given the expansion of digital marketing ecosystems in developing markets, there is a strong 
demand for solutions that align with the operational realities of smaller businesses.

4. Literature Review 
The following section reviews existing research and industry insights relevant to the proposed 
system, while also identifying limitations that the project aims to address. 
• NLP-Based Hashtag Generation: 
Prior research introduces transformer-based architectures for generating hashtags from 
textual data. These models improve upon traditional sequence-to-sequence approaches 
by focusing on more relevant segments of input text. However, such methods typically 
operate only on text and do not incorporate additional contextual signals such as visual 
content or compliance constraints. The proposed system extends this approach by 
combining text with image inputs and validating outputs against a restricted hashtag 
dataset [1].  
• Multimodal Hashtag Prediction: 
Studies using multimodal transformers demonstrate that combining image and caption 
data leads to more accurate hashtag predictions compared to text -only models. While 
these findings highlight the effectiveness of joint learning, they are generally limited to 
experimental settings. The proposed system builds upon this concept by embedding it 
within a real-time workflow that allows users to upload content and instantly receive 
results [3].  
• Engagement Prediction Using Visual Features: 
Existing work shows that visual characteristics of social media posts can be used to 
estimate engagement levels. However, many of these models are developed within 
narrow domains and lack adaptability across different industries. The proposed 
approach addresses this limitation by applying time -series forecasting techniques to 
support broader and more flexible predictions [2].  
• Computer Vision in Marketing Analysis: 
Research identifies computer vision as a useful tool for analyzing visual elements such 
as objects, logos, and color patterns in marketing content. Despite this, practical 
systems for continuously tracking brand consistency over time are not widely 
implemented. The proposed solution introduces an automated mechanism for detecting 
variations in visual identity across posts [4].  
• Automated Compliance Systems: 
Current moderation technologies are primarily designed to enforce platform policies at 
scale. These systems focus on detecting violations after content is published rather than

5. Objectives 
The project objectives are defined using measurable criteria to ensure clear evaluation of 
system performance and usability. 
 
1. Brand Visual Auditing: 
Develop a module that analyzes uploaded images to detect inconsistencies in brand 
color schemes. The system will apply OpenCV and K -Means clustering in HSV color 
space, targeting a palette comparison accuracy of at least 90% when tested on a 
validation dataset of 500 branded images.  
 
2. Hashtag Generation (NLP Module): 
Design a caption -to-hashtag generation model using fine -tuned transformer 
architectures such as DistilBERT or RoBERTa. The model will be trained on a dataset 
of over 10,000 Instagram captions and is expected to generate a minimum of 10 relevant 
hashtags pe r input, achieving a BLEU score of at least 0.45 compared to human -
generated references.  
 
3. Compliance Validation: 
Implement an automated system to identify restricted or inappropriate hashtags by 
integrating a continuously updated database. The system will utilize web scraping 
workflows (via n8n) and aim to achieve a recall rate of at least 95% in detecting policy 
violations.  
 
4. Engagement Forecasting: 
 
Develop a predictive module using time -series models such as ARIMA or Prophet to 
estimate engagement trends. The system will generate performance forecasts with a 
Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of 15% or lower when compared to historical data

6. Scope and Limitations 
In-Scope 
• Instagram and Facebook as target platforms for compliance and scheduling modules. 
• Image-based brand auditing: colour palette extraction, font inconsistency flagging, and 
design fatigue detection. 
• Caption-to-hashtag generation for text-based captions submitted by users. 
• Banned/restricted hashtag compliance checking for Instagram community guidelines. 
• Engagement trend prediction based on historical post data provided by users. 
• A web-based React.js dashboard for SME users to upload media and view results. 
Out-of-Scope 
• IoT hardware integration, physical sensors, or real-time streaming camera inputs. 
• TikTok, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, or YouTube platform -specific compliance rules (future 
work). 
• Direct API publishing to social platforms (regulatory/API-tier limitation). 
• Multilingual NLP support beyond English in this iteration. 
• Custom model training on client-specific brand datasets (requires data volume beyond 
FYP scope). 
Assumptions 
• Users will provide their own historical engagement data in CSV format for forecasting. 
• The banned hashtag database will be maintained via n8n -driven web scraping on a 
weekly update cycle.

7. Methodology 
The project follows an Agile -Iterative Software Development model with four parallel 
development tracks corresponding to each system module. Integration testing is performed at 
the end of each sprint. 
Phase 1 - Research and Data Collection 
• Literature review of 5 core research papers and industry reports (complete). 
• Dataset curation: Instagram image -caption pairs for NLP training; historical 
engagement CSVs for forecasting model. 
• Tool setup: Python environment, Hugging Face Transformers, OpenCV, Django/React 
scaffold. 
Phase 2 - Computer Vision Module 
• Implement image preprocessing pipeline using OpenCV (resize, normalise, HSV 
conversion). 
• Apply K-Means clustering to extract dominant colour palettes from uploaded images. 
• Build palette comparison algorithm using Euclidean distance in HSV space; flag 
deviations beyond variance threshold. 
• Integrate YOLO-based object detection for logo/text region identification. 
• Evaluation metric: Intersection over Union (IoU) for region detection; palette 
comparison accuracy on labelled dataset. 
Phase 3 - NLP Module 
• Fine-tune DistilBERT on curated Instagram caption-hashtag dataset (minimum 10,000 
pairs). 
• Implement multimodal extension: image features from ResNet -50 concatenated with 
text embeddings. 
• Build banned-hashtag cross-reference layer using compliance database. 
• Evaluation metric: BLEU score against human-generated hashtag sets. 
Phase 4 - Forecasting & Compliance Modules 
• Implement ARIMA/Prophet time-series model on user-provided historical engagement 
data. 
• Build n8n automation workflow for weekly banned -hashtag database refresh via web 
scraping. 
• Integrate compliance checker with NLP output: flag generated hashtags before they 
reach the user.

12. Conclusion 
The proposed platform aims to simplify how SMEs manage social media content by bringing 
together several essential functions into a unified system. By combining image-based analysis, 
language processing, compliance validation, and predictive modeling, it a ddresses 
inefficiencies associated with fragmented workflows. 
While each individual component is supported by existing research, the primary value lies in 
integrating these capabilities into a single, practical application. Many currently available tools 
either focus on limited aspects of content management or remain  financially inaccessible to 
smaller organizations. 
As the use of AI in marketing continues to expand, there is a growing need for solutions that 
are both effective and accessible. This project contributes toward that objective by offering a 
system designed with the constraints and requirements of SMEs in mind. 
Future developments could include support for additional platforms, multilingual processing, 
and further refinement of predictive models to improve adaptability across different industries.
